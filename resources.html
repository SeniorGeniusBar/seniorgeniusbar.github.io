Built-In Tools Worth Knowing

  • Find My / Find My Device — helps locate a misplaced phone or tablet.
  • Magnifier or camera zoom — helps with menus, labels, mail, and small print.
  • Accessibility settings — can make text larger, bolder, higher contrast, or read aloud.

Use Trusted Sources

If a website, pop-up, phone call, or text tells you that your device has a virus or asks you to pay for urgent technical help, stop before clicking or paying. Use an official support site or ask Aiden a question first.
