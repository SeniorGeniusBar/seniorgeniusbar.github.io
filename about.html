About Senior Genius Bar

Helping older adults become more confident with technology through patient, one-on-one support.

Meet Aiden

Hi, I'm Aiden O'Neill, a local high school student and founder of Senior Genius Bar. I started this project after seeing how a little patience and clear instruction could make technology feel less overwhelming for older adults. Since then, I've volunteered with older relatives, neighbors, residents at Atria Senior Living, and members of my community, helping them become more confident using the technology they already own.

I also designed and built this website so seniors and their families can access technology guides, troubleshooting resources, and request help between weekly in-person sessions.

Why I Started It

Over and over, I noticed the same thing: most technology isn't actually difficult—it just feels unfamiliar at first. What helps isn't doing it for someone, but meeting them where they are and explaining each step clearly, patiently, and without judgment.

Senior Genius Bar is meant to provide calm, patient support and easy resources seniors can use between in-person visits.

How I Try to Teach

I've always paid close attention to small details that often go unnoticed — and I think that's what makes step-by-step teaching work. I try never to skip a step, even one that seems obvious, since it's usually the skipped step that causes the most confusion.

Beyond Weekly Visits

Senior Genius Bar is more than a weekly help session. I created this website so older adults and their families can continue learning between visits through step-by-step guides, helpful resources, and an easy way to request assistance whenever they need it.
