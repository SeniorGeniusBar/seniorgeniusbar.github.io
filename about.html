About Senior Genius Bar

A student-founded volunteer program helping older adults become more confident with technology through patient, one-on-one support.

Meet Aiden

Hi, I'm Aiden O'Neill, a local high school student and founder of Senior Genius Bar. I started this project after seeing how a little patience and clear instruction could make technology feel less overwhelming for older adults. Since then, I've volunteered with older relatives, neighbors, residents at Atria Senior Living, and members of my community, helping them become more confident using the technology they already own.

I also designed and built this website so seniors and their families can access technology guides, troubleshooting resources, and request help between in-person sessions.

Why I Started It

Over and over, I noticed the same thing: most technology isn't actually difficult—it just feels unfamiliar at first. What helps isn't doing it for someone, but meeting people where they are and explaining each step clearly, patiently, and without judgment.

Senior Genius Bar exists to provide calm, patient support and easy-to-follow resources that help older adults become more independent and confident using technology.

The program continues through community visits, online guides, and email support, so people can get help in the way that is most comfortable for them.

How I Try to Teach

I've learned that most technology problems come from one missed step or one unfamiliar setting—not from a lack of ability. That's why I slow down, explain every step clearly, and make sure each person understands how to solve the problem independently. My goal isn't just to fix a device—it's to build confidence.

Beyond In-Person Visits

Senior Genius Bar is more than an in-person help session. I created this website so older adults, their families, and community members can continue learning through step-by-step technology guides, helpful resources, and an easy way to request assistance whenever they need it.

About This Website

I designed and developed this website to extend Senior Genius Bar beyond in-person visits. It provides technology guides, accessibility resources, and a simple way for seniors, families, and community members to request help remotely while reinforcing the same patient, step-by-step teaching approach I use during every volunteer session.
