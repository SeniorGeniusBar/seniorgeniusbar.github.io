Accessibility & Comfort

Make Your Device Easier to See & Use

These settings are built into most phones and tablets. Change one thing at a time, and keep whichever setting feels best to you.

aA1Make Text LargerMake words easier to read.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Accessibility.
  3. Tap Display & Text Size.
  4. Tap Larger Text.
  5. Move the slider until the words are comfortable to read.
  6. Need it even larger? Turn on Larger Accessibility Sizes.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Use the Settings search and type Font size.
  3. Move the slider until the words are comfortable to read.
  4. You can also search for Display size to make buttons and other items larger.
Aiden's Tip: Choose the size that is easiest for you to read. There is no “right” size.
Still stuck? Ask Aiden
B2Turn On Bold TextMake letters stand out more clearly.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Accessibility.
  3. Tap Display & Text Size.
  4. Turn on Bold Text.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Search for Bold text or open Accessibility.
  3. On many phones, look under Display size and text or Vision enhancements.
  4. Turn on the bold-text option if your phone offers it.
Aiden's Tip: Try larger text and bold text together. Many people find that combination easier to read.

⌕3Use Your Phone as a MagnifierSee menus, labels, mail, and other small print.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Find and open the Magnifier app. If you cannot find it, swipe down on the Home Screen and search for “Magnifier.”
  2. Point the camera at what you want to read.
  3. Use the zoom control to make it larger.
  4. You can also adjust brightness or freeze the image if that makes it easier to see.

Android

  1. For small print in the real world, open the Camera and use the zoom controls.
  2. To enlarge something already on your screen, open Settings and search for Magnification.
  3. Turn on the magnification shortcut your phone offers.
Aiden's Tip: This is especially useful for menus, appliance buttons, medicine labels, and other tiny print. For medical instructions, double-check anything that is hard to read with a pharmacist or healthcare professional.
Still stuck? Ask Aiden
◐4Make Your Screen Easier to SeeImprove contrast and reduce distracting backgrounds.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Accessibility.
  3. Tap Display & Text Size.
  4. Try Increase Contrast.
  5. You can also try Reduce Transparency.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Search for contrast, high contrast text, or color correction.
  3. Your phone may place these settings under Accessibility, Vision enhancements, or Display size and text.
  4. Try one setting at a time and keep the one that is easiest to see.
Aiden's Tip: Change one setting at a time so you can tell whether it actually helps. You can always turn it back off.
Still stuck? Ask Aiden
🔊5Have Your Device Read to YouHear words on the screen spoken aloud.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Accessibility.
  3. Look for Spoken Content or Read & Speak. The name can vary by software version.
  4. Turn on Speak Screen or the read-aloud option you prefer.
  5. Follow the on-screen directions for starting read-aloud.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Accessibility.
  3. Look for Select to Speak or search Settings for “read aloud.”
  4. Turn the feature on and follow the on-screen directions.
Aiden's Tip: If the menu name on your device looks different, use the search box inside Settings and type “speak,” “read aloud,” or “accessibility.”
