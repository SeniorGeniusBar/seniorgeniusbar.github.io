Before You Reach Out

A Few Things That Help

  • Have your device charged and turned on.
  • Describe what happened in your own words — for example, “the screen went black” or “I can't find my photos.”
  • If it's easy, a photo or screenshot of the screen can help.
  • Never send passwords, verification codes, banking information, Social Security numbers, or medical information.

Try It Yourself

Quick Tips & Common Fixes

A few things you can often solve in under a minute. If the steps don't match what you see, stop and ask — devices can look different.

My screen is too dim, or the text is too small

On many phones and tablets, swipe down from the top of the screen to find brightness controls. For text size, look in Settings under Display or Accessibility for “Text Size” or “Larger Text.”

See the full picture guides →

My phone keeps buzzing with notifications

Open Settings → Notifications. You can usually turn notifications down app by app without changing the rest of your phone.

I can't hear my phone ring or my videos

Check the physical volume buttons first and make sure the device isn't in silent mode. Then check the sound settings on the device.

I can't find a photo I know I took

Open the Photos app and look in Recents or All Photos. If it still isn't there, ask for help before changing or deleting anything.

I forgot my password or can't log in

Look for a “Forgot Password” link on the sign-in screen. Avoid guessing repeatedly because some accounts temporarily lock after too many attempts. Never send your password to Senior Genius Bar.

An app or update is asking me to do something confusing

It's okay to stop before tapping anything you are unsure about, especially if it asks for a password, payment, verification code, or personal information.

Good to Know

Common Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. Senior Genius Bar is a volunteer project. Basic remote technology help is free as Aiden's schedule allows. In-person sessions are offered through the community pages shown on this site.

What if I don't know how to take a screenshot?

That's completely fine. Describe what you see in your own words, or request a call. There is no need to know technical terms.

What kinds of problems can be solved remotely?

Many simple problems can be handled remotely: text size, sound, photos, video calls, settings, notifications, and explaining a confusing screen. More involved issues may be better handled in person.

Will you ever ask for my password or control my device?

No. Senior Genius Bar will never ask you to send a password, verification code, banking information, or Social Security number, and will not ask to remotely control your device.
